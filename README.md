# Bank-Marketing-Campaign-
Campaign Analysis 
💹    The current campaign has a 12% conversion rate (customers agree to the term deposit). In contrast, the success rate from past marketing campaigns was very low, at only 3%.
🧓   Between the ages of 25 and 40, they have subscribed to a term deposit, and most of them are married couples. 
💸   Most term deposit subscribers have low and average account balances and no personal loans, but some have housing debt. 
🏦   Only 1% has credit defaults, and some of them agree to a term deposit. 
📞   The average call duration to explain this campaign to customers is around 5 minutes. 
💁   There is no positive correlation between contact attempts and conversion rate.
